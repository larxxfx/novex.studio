# Engagement checklist (every script)

- [ ] **0–2 s:** intriguing question, surprising statement or visual mystery — not a logo, not a greeting
- [ ] **Curiosity gap** opened in the first 5 s; the answer is NOT given immediately
- [ ] **Progressive reveals:** at least 3 new pieces of information, each raising the stakes
- [ ] **One unexpected turn** (the early-NØVEX "real shock" beat)
- [ ] **Question to the viewer** at a natural point, specific to the subject (e.g. "Would you explore this place?", "YES or NO?")
- [ ] **Payoff** that rewards watching to the end
- [ ] Closing question only if it invites a real opinion/prediction/choice
- [ ] No misleading clickbait: the title's promise is delivered in the video
- [ ] No generic "like and subscribe" filler
- [ ] Mode is honest: REALITY sourced · SPECULATION labelled · FICTION labelled
