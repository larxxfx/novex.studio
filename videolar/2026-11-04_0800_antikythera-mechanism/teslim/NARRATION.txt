1. [merak (kanca)] In nineteen-oh-one, sponge divers pulled a lump of corroded bronze from a Roman-era shipwreck.
2. [şaşkınlık] Inside it were gears.
3. [hayranlık] At least thirty of them, cut by hand, more than two thousand years ago.
4. [açılım] This is the Antikythera Mechanism.
5. [keşif] Turn a handle, and it showed the Sun, the Moon, and the planets,
6. [hayranlık] predicted eclipses, and even tracked the dates of the Olympic Games.
7. [gizem] Only about a third of it survived, in eighty-two fragments.
8. [güven / kanıt] In twenty twenty-one, a team at University College London rebuilt its front display from X-ray scans.
9. [doruk] Nothing this complex appears again for more than a thousand years.
10. [soru / katılım] So was it one of a kind, or the only one that survived? And which lost technology should we uncover next: Damascus steel, or Roman concrete?
