1. [şaşkınlık (kanca)] On Venus, a single day lasts longer than an entire year.
2. [merak] Venus takes two hundred and twenty-five Earth days to orbit the Sun.
3. [açılım] But it takes two hundred and forty-three Earth days to spin around just once.
4. [hayranlık] No planet in the solar system spins slower.
5. [şaşkınlık] And it spins backwards.
6. [merak] So on Venus, the Sun rises in the west, and sets in the east.
7. [açıklama] From one sunrise to the next takes about a hundred and seventeen Earth days.
8. [hayranlık] Just two sunrises in a whole Venus year.
9. [gerilim] Under clouds of sulfuric acid, at around four hundred and sixty-five degrees Celsius.
10. [soru / katılım] Could you survive a single day on Venus? And which planet should we explore next: Mars, or Mercury?
