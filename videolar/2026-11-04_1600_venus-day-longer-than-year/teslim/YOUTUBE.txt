TITLE: On Venus, a Day Is Longer Than a Year

DESCRIPTION:
Venus orbits the Sun in about 225 Earth days, but needs about 243 Earth days to spin once on its axis. So a Venus day (one rotation) is longer than its year.

It also spins backwards (retrograde), so the Sun rises in the west and sets in the east. Combined with its orbit, sunrise to sunrise takes about 117 Earth days: just two sunrises per Venus year, under sulfuric acid clouds at around 465 °C.
