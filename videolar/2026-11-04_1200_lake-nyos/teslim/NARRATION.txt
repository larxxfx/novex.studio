1. [gerilim (kanca)] On the night of August twenty-first, nineteen eighty-six, a lake in the hills of Cameroon exhaled.
2. [gizem] There was no fire. No flood. No explosion.
3. [gizem / gerilim] Just a silent cloud of carbon dioxide, rising from the bottom of Lake Nyos.
4. [gerilim] The gas was heavier than air.
5. [gerilim] It flowed down the valleys, into the villages, while people slept.
6. [doruk / hüzün] By morning, one thousand seven hundred and forty-six people were dead, and three thousand five hundred animals.
7. [açıklama] Volcanic gas had been slowly building up deep in the water, until the lake could not hold it.
8. [umut] Today, pipes vent that gas safely, shooting fountains high above the lake.
9. [gizem] And just two years earlier, nearby Lake Monoun had done the same.
10. [soru / katılım] Would you live beside a lake that can breathe? And which deadly place on Earth should we explore next?
