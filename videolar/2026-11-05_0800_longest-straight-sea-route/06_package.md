## Title (YouTube Shorts / TikTok / Reels)
This Line Never Touches Land for 32,000 km

## Description
Point a ship west from Pakistan and keep going straight: you won't hit land for 32,090 km, until Kamchatka.
On a flat map the route curves; on a globe it's a perfect straight line.
Source: Chabukswar & Mukherjee, "Longest Straight Line Paths on Water or Land on the Earth" (arXiv:1804.07389, 2018).
