## Description
Point a ship west from Pakistan and never turn the wheel. For 32,090 km you won't touch land: you slip between Africa and Madagascar, pass between South America and Antarctica, cross the entire Pacific, and finally reach Russia's Kamchatka Peninsula.

On a flat map the route looks like a snake. On a globe, it's one perfectly straight line (a great circle).

In 2018, researchers Rohan Chabukswar and Kushal Mukherjee used a computer algorithm to confirm it is the longest straight-line path on water. The longest straight line on land? Portugal to China, about 11,241 km.

Could a real ship sail it without stopping? Yes or no? Tell us in the comments.

Source: Chabukswar & Mukherjee, "Longest Straight Line Paths on Water or Land on the Earth", arXiv:1804.07389 (2018).
